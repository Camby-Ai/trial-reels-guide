01 / What a trial reel is

Strangers first.
Followers later.

A trial reel is an Instagram feature. Instagram shows the reel to people who do not follow you, and keeps it off your grid and out of your followers' feeds. If it does well, you share it to everyone. If it does not, your followers never saw it.

That is why this works for a business. You can test five versions of one idea in a week without wearing out the audience you already have.

  • Who can use it. A public account with at least 200 followers on a professional account (business or creator), or 1,000 on a personal one.
  • When you see numbers. Insights show up within 24 hours of sharing.
  • How a winner gets posted. Open the reel, tap View insights, tap Share to everyone. Or turn on Share to everyone automatically in the trial settings and Instagram does it when the reel is performing well.
  • What it does not hide. A follower can still run into a trial reel through a DM, an audio page or search. Do not post anything as a trial that you would not post for real.

The rules are Instagram's and they change. The current ones are in the Instagram Help Center.

03 / The setup

Five steps.
In this order.

01

Check your Instagram before you sign up for anything

The free week starts the moment you create the Ripping account. Do not spend two of the seven days finding out your account cannot post trial reels.

  • Switch to a professional account. It is free and it is in Instagram's settings. Business or creator both work.
  • Make sure the account is public.
  • Count your followers. You need 200 on a professional account.
  • Open Account Status in settings. An account with limits on its reach loses trial reels until Instagram can recommend it again.
02

Get Claude Code running

Ripping does its work through Claude Code, so that has to be installed and signed in first. Get it from claude.com/claude-code, sign in with your Claude plan, and ask it one question to confirm it answers.

03

Start the Ripping trial

Go to getripping.com, choose Get Ripping and create your account.

Set a reminder for day 6

When the trial ends, the paid plan starts by itself. Ripping's refund policy says the charge made when a trial converts is not refundable. If it is not for you, cancel from the Billing page before then.

04

Connect Claude and Instagram

Ripping's own onboarding walks you through both. I am not copying their screens here. They ship updates often (version 2.1.2 when I wrote this), so their steps will be more current than mine.

  • Connect one Instagram account to start. The plan allows up to ten. One is enough to learn what works.
  • Connect the account you sell from. Not a personal one you are nervous about.
05

Make your first reel a trial

Ripping works in six stages. For the first reel you only need four of them.

  • Swipe. Look through the reels winning in your niche and save the ones closest to what you sell.
  • Script. Turn one into a script in your own words. Read every line. Fix anything that is not true about your business.
  • Produce. Pick a style and let it build the video.
  • Post. Send it out as a trial reel, not to your feed.

The last two stages, Analyze and Repurpose, are for tomorrow, once Instagram has numbers.

Read before it goes out

A trial reel is hidden from your followers. It is not hidden from the public. A wrong price or a promise you cannot keep is still out there with your name on it.

Views tell you the hook worked. DMs, calls and form fills tell you the offer worked. A reel with fewer views and three people asking for a quote beat the one with ten times the views and none.
